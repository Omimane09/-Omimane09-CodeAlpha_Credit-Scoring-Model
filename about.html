About CreditScoringAI

A professional-grade Machine Learning credit risk assessment system

Objective

CreditScoringAI predicts whether a loan applicant is Low Risk, Medium Risk, or High Risk using advanced machine learning techniques. It analyzes 24+ financial features including income, debt, savings, credit history, and payment behavior.

How It Works

The system trains and compares five models — Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and XGBoost — then automatically selects and saves the best-performing model. Each prediction returns risk level, probabilities, confidence, and personalized recommendations.
